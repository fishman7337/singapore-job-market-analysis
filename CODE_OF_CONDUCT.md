# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project should remain a respectful academic and technical space. Contributors are expected to communicate constructively, explain trade-offs clearly, and treat different levels of experience with patience.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Give feedback that focuses on the work, evidence, and project goals.
- Respect academic integrity, data privacy, and source attribution.
- Be transparent when analysis assumptions are uncertain.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Misrepresentation of data sources, authorship, or results.
- Publishing private, sensitive, or unauthorised data.
- Deliberately weakening tests, documentation, or reproducibility controls.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from further participation.
