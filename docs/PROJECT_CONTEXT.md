# Project Context

This repository was produced for Singapore Polytechnic, School of Computing, under the Diploma in Applied AI & Analytics.

## Module Details

- Module: ST1510 Programming for Data Analytics
- Assessment: CA2
- Academic year: AY2024/2025, Year 1 Semester 2
- Students: Goh Kun Ming and Goh Jenson
- Lecturer: Senior Lecturer Goh Chia Ming

## Project Title

Singapore Graduate Employment, Salary & Labour-Market Analysis

## Research Focus

The project studies how degree specialisation, industry, gender, and labour market competitiveness relate to employment and salary outcomes in Singapore.

## Intended Audience

- Academic markers reviewing the CA2 submission
- Students learning how to structure a data analytics project
- Portfolio reviewers assessing analytics, Python, and DataOps practices
- Future maintainers who may reproduce or extend the analysis
